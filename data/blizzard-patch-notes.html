Reign of Talon - Season 5: A Grim Doctrine

New Event - Mystery Mayhem: Graveyard Games

A freakishly fun event rises from the grave -- Mystery Madness: Graveyard Games!

For four wicked weeks, complete challenges to earn ravishing rewards, and cast Offerings to help determine which shadowy characters are first to be summoned into a brand new limited-time mode.

Ghoulishly Good Challenges

Complete challenges to collect rewards. Every challenge conquered unearths spooky swag, as well as Offerings you can use in voting.

Offer Up Your Devotion

Offerings decide which characters crawl into the Graveyard Games mode next. Earn as many Offerings as you can, and direct them to any of the available heroes you want; you can spread them out, or pledge your allegiance to just one!

New Arcade Gamemode - Mystery Madness: Graveyard Games

Mystery Madness is our new Halloween Event mode, combining elements of Mystery Heroes, Junkenstein’s Lab, and Stadium. But there's an added twist: you gain Powers when you die. Mystery Madness is 5v5 Open Queue but with guaranteed 1-2-2 Role Composition and has the game format of Best-of-3 Control.

At the start of a match, you are assigned a random role and given 3 hero choices within that role. If you like to leave your chances up to fate, there is also a random button that will pick between 3 heroes and grant you a small boost.

Once the match starts, you will gain experience passively and from eliminations and assists. Once you gain enough experience, you will earn a level. When you die or on round transition, you will earn Power Choices, picking between 2 Powers, equal to the number of levels you accumulated since you last died. Powers are similar to Perks or Stadium Items / Powers, and can be either Common, Epic, or Legendary. Commons are comparable to Minor Perks or Rare to Epic Stadium items, and are usually supportive effects. Epics are comparable to Major Perks or Stadium Powers, and are usually the cornerstone of your build. Legendaries go even further beyond, and can lead to some truly crazy combinations. Some standout spooky powers are Jump Scare, which turns you into a Prop while out of combat, letting you flank and frighten your enemies, or Zomnic Summoner, which lets you throw a grenade that summons a Zomnic upon detonation. On death, you will also gain 10% Ultimate Charge and respawn with Overhealth equal to 40% of your Max Life and 30% Move Speed while out of combat.

Our newest spooky support, Doctrine, will also be playable in Mystery Madness: Graveyard Games!

General Updates

Custom Restricted Words
  • Added new Option (Custom Restricted Words) to Social -> Text Chat.
  • Added information regarding Custom Restricted Words to Text Profanity Filters info dialog and Report dialog.
  • Player-sent chat messages containing a restricted word(s) will be hidden with a "Possible Bad Message (Custom)" warning, with the option to reveal the message.

Unvaulted Battle Passes

You can now progress Unvaulted Battle Passes from past seasons alongside the current season's Battle Pass!

  • From the Home Screen, navigate to Battle Passes > Legacy Pass to see the full list of available Passes.

  • Previously purchased Battle Passes can be activated and progressed. Any progress that was made when the Battle Pass was initially run is carried over when the Pass becomes unvaulted.

    • 2026: Season 4 Battle Pass will not be available to activate at launch and will be made available in the midseason.
    • Certain rewards such as Mythic Skins, Mythic Prisms, Overwatch Coins, XP Boosts, and Player Titles are no longer obtainable in unvaulted passes.

  • Legacy Seasons 1-15 Battle Passes are available to purchase in the shop under the new Unvaulted Battle Pass Tab.

    • Locked Unvaulted Passes will arrive in later seasons.

  • In order to earn Battle Pass XP towards an Unvaulted Pass, you must own it and then activate it from the Legacy Pass Menu.

    • Only 1 Unvaulted Pass can be active at a time to progress alongside the current season's Battle Pass.

Competitive Play Updates

Top 500 Challenger System Updates
  • Top 500 Challenger Leaderboard sorting default changed to Rank (from Challenger Score).
  • End of Season title rewards are now referencing Rank instead of Challenger Score.
  • Removed Heat Bonus.

Developer Comments: This greater focus on Rank over Challenger Score is intended to reward our most skilled players with less of a focus on play time. Challenger Score remains only as a requirement to be on the leaderboard. Only the 500 players with the highest Challenger Score will be on the Leaderboard, but it's now possible to be Rank 1 with the lowest Challenger Score of those 500 players if you have the highest actual Rank.  We believe this new direction will still prevent high ranked accounts with very low playtime from being on the Leaderboard, while now more accurately rewarding the best players that play throughout a season.

Hero Reworks

Roadhog

Developer Comments: We had two main goals with this Roadhog rework: to reduce the frustration that comes out of how lethal Chain Hook ends up being on most heroes, and to give Roadhog a new tool to help him aid his team as a more traditional Tank would. Changing his primary fire to shoot in a 2-burst cadence alongside slight adjustments to Chain Hook should preserve the ability’s strength as a displacement tool while dampening its lethality. Meanwhile, Roadhog’s new ability, Trash Compactor, brings new utility for him and his team that feeds back into Roadhog being a lethal force on the battlefield. While Trash Compactor is active, Roadhog vacuums up projectiles in front of him, converting them into an explosive projectile to fling back at enemies. Operating as a strong defensive and offensive ability, Trash Compactor should help point Roadhog towards making game-swinging plays with more vectors than just trying to one-shot-combo heroes.

Sombra

Developer Comments: Sombra is being brought into the Support role with this latest rework. In the past, her identity as a disruptive mastermind was at odds with the value a Damage hero was expected to bring. Moving Sombra to the Support role should allow us to fully lean into her hero fantasy while keeping important pillars of her gameplay intact.

One notable change of the rework is the removal of Hack as an ability. Facing Sombra often meant being unexpectedly locked out of your abilities from time to time. While this can create fun moments of cancelling key abilities for the Sombra player, most players found it jarring, frustrating, and difficult to deal with. By removing Hack from her main abilities, we brought in a new debuff Sombra can inflict on enemies: Weaken, which halves an enemy’s own damage and healing output. A well-timed weaken can turn the tide of battle, much like Hack was able to, without completely locking an enemy out of their own abilities.

Hero Updates

Tank

D.Mon

Developer Comments: D.Mon has settled into a strong position since launch. We're reducing Call Mech's transformation time to match D.Va's while adjusting Portable Fusion Repeater to improve accuracy and reduce effectiveness at longer distances. We're also reducing Plasma Saber's damage to help offset these improvements.

  • Armor reduced from 325 to 300. (6v6)
Plasma Saber
  • Damage reduced from 65 to 60.
Power Barrier
  • Cast time decreased from 0.065s to 0.016s.
  • Notches on side of shield reduced in size.
Call Mech
  • Transformation duration has been reduced from 1.95 to 0.95 seconds.
  • A new first-person bootup animation lasting 1 second has been added.
Portable Fusion Repeater
  • Spread reduced from 2 to 1.75 degrees.
  • Minimum falloff range reduced from 30 to 20 meters.
D.Va

Developer Comments: We're increasing D.Va's base health to improve her durability without making adjustments to her Defense Matrix.

  • Mech base health increased from 175 to 200. (5v5)
Domina

Developer Comments: Domina's ultimate is disruptive but not always consequential relative to the time required to earn it. We're reducing its cost to make it available more frequently.

  • Ultimate Cost reduced by 6%.
Mauga

Developer Comments: Mauga continues to have high durability and damage output. We're reverting the spread adjustment made to single-fire Chainguns while reducing the effectiveness of firing both weapons at longer ranges. Alongside his armor reduction, these changes will lower both survivability and lethality.

  • Base armor reduced from 150 to 125.
Chainguns
  • Maximum spread when firing a single gun reverted from 1.5 to 1 degree.
  • Maximum spread when firing both guns increased from 4 to 5 degrees.
  • Damage falloff minimum range when firing both guns reduced from 15 to 10 meters.
Winston

Developer Comments: Winston's Barrier Projector cooldown is being reduced to shorten the downtime between engagements. To offset the increased availability, its duration is also being reduced.

Barrier Projector
  • Cooldown reduced from 12 to 10 seconds. (5v5)
  • Duration reduced from 8 to 7 seconds.
wrecking Ball

Developer Comments: Adaptive Barrier has provided Wrecking Ball with too long a protection window. We're reducing its duration to place greater emphasis on timing its use.

Adaptive Barrier – Major Perk

  • Duration reduced from 1.5 to 1 second.
Zarya

Developer Comments: The cooldown of Particle Barrier is being increased to reduce Zarya's personal barrier uptime and energy generation. Projected Barrier remains unchanged, encouraging more proactive use on allies.

Particle Barrier
  • Cooldown increased from 11 to 12 seconds. (5v5)

Damage

Anran

Developer Comments: Dancing Blaze can generate significant visual clutter, making Anran difficult to track during combat. We're removing the visual clones visible to opponents to improve clarity. Vermillion Revival's cast time is also being reduced to shorten the window opponents have to react.

Dancing Blaze
  • Reduced the amount of visual effects enemies see when impacted by Dancing Blaze.
Vermillion Revival
  • Cast time reduced from 3 to 2.5 seconds.
Freja

Developer Comments: Recent changes have significantly increased Freja's damage output. We're reducing the damage of both Revdraw Crossbow and Take Aim, while also lowering the effectiveness of Relentless Barrage. Explosive Bolt's impact sound effects have also been reduced to lessen audio clutter when repeatedly hit.

Relentless Barrage – Minor Perk

  • Refunded automatic bolts reduced from 8 to 5.
Revdraw Crossbow
  • Damage reduced from 30 to 28.
Take Aim
  • Explosive Bolt explosion damage reduced from 80 to 75.
  • Explosive bolt impact sound effects reduced.
Junkrat

Developer Comments: Junkrat has struggled to remain effective since the weakening of his Concussion Mine combo. We're reducing Concussion Mine's cooldown to increase both damage output and mobility. Nitro Boost's movement speed bonus is also being reduced to limit the high speeds of the ultimate with the perk equipped.

Nitro Boost – Minor Perk

  • Bonus speed reduced from 125% to 100%.
Concussion Mine
  • Cooldown reduced from 8 to 7 seconds.
Sierra

Developer Comments: Sierra's damage output has remained high since release, but her weapon reliability has been limited by projectile travel time at longer ranges. We're reducing the damage of both Helix Rifle and Tracking Shot while increasing projectile speed to improve weapon responsiveness and consistency. Tremor Charge's cooldown is also being reduced to make it available more frequently.

Helix Rifle
  • Weapon projectile speed increased from 90 to 120.
  • Damage reduced from 9 to 8.5.
Tracking Shot
  • Damage reduced from 9 to 8.5.
Tremor Charge
  • Cooldown reduced from 8.5 to 7 seconds.
Symmetra

Developer Comments: Symmetra's Teleporter can be extremely effective in coordinated teams, enabling long-distance repositioning with limited opportunities for opponents to respond. We're reducing its placement range to limit how far teams can travel with it, while increasing the reuse cooldown to make Teleporter use a greater commitment.

Teleporter
  • Placement range reduced from 30 to 25 meters.
  • Teleport reuse cooldown increased from 1 to 1.25 seconds.
Torbjörn

Developer Comments: Torbjörn's secondary fire is being improved to increase its consistency at closer ranges. We're also reducing Anchor Bolts' turret throw range to preserve the perk's flexibility while limiting extremely distant turret placements.

Anchor Bolts – Major Perk

  • Deploy turrets increased throw range reduced from 50% to 25%.
Rivet Gun
  • Secondary fire spread reduced from 4.5 to 4 degrees.
Vendetta

Developer Comments: These changes are intended to improve the responsiveness of Vendetta's primary fire without significantly increasing the speed of her overall combo. We've reduced delays within Palatine Fang while increasing the recovery times of Whirlwind Dash and Sundering Blade to preserve overall pacing.

Palatine Fang
  • Initial Swing delay reduced from 0.3 to 0.15 seconds.
  • Horizontal swing duration reduced from 0.25 to 0.2 seconds.
  • Combo link duration increased from 0.75 to 0.9 seconds.
Whirlwind Dash
  • Recovery increased from 0.5 to 0.75 seconds.
Sundering Blade
  • Recovery increased from 0.2 to 0.5 seconds.
  • Camera pitch is no longer fully locked during the slash.
Widowmaker
Take Aim
  • Updated Widowmaker's Take Aim charge sound effects for the default, Mythic, and Hello Kitty weapons. These sound effects now increase in intensity when the charge level is above 50%.

Support

Baptiste

Developer Comments: Immortality Field's long cooldown and vulnerability to being destroyed can limit its effectiveness. We're increasing its health, lowering its cooldown, and raising its minimum health threshold to improve its overall reliability.

Immortality Field
  • Unkillable health threshold increased from 20% to 25%.
  • Cooldown reduced from 22 to 20 seconds.
  • Health increased from 125 to 150.
Brigitte

Developer Comments: Brigitte's durability can be limited due to operating at close range. We're shifting a portion of her health into armor to improve survivability.

  • Health reduced from 200 to 175.
  • Armor increased from 50 to 75.
Jetpack Cat

Developer Comments: Recent movement changes have significantly improved Jetpack Cat's mobility and overall effectiveness. We're reducing the damage and healing of Biotic Pawjectiles to lower overall output. Lifeline is also being adjusted to provide additional counterplay against carried targets while improving its reliability when rescuing allies from environmental deaths.

  • Increased sound priority and volume of base movement and Frenetic Flight.
Biotic Pawjectiles
  • Damage and healing per projectile reduced from 4.5 to 4.
Lifeline
  • Stuns and mobility locking effects on a carried target now disconnect Lifeline.
  • Allies now automatically accept Lifeline when over a death plane.
Kiriko

Developer Comments: Kiriko has become highly effective as a healer while retaining the utility and mobility of her kit. We're reducing the speed and range of Healing Ofuda to lower her healing consistency at longer distances. Protection Suzu is also being adjusted to reduce the effectiveness of its invulnerability and require more precise use.

Healing Ofuda
  • Projectile travel speed reduced from 24 to 18 meters per second.
  • Maximum seeking range reduced from 35 to 30 meters.
Protection Suzu
  • Phased out invulnerability duration reduced from 0.65 to 0.5 seconds.
  • Explosion radius reduced from 5 to 4 meters.
Wuyang

Developer Comments: Wuyang's empowered healing resource can be limiting when responding to sustained damage. We're increasing its regeneration rate to provide more frequent access to empowered healing.

Restorative Stream
  • Regeneration rate increased from 12.5% to 15% per second.
  • Line of sight timeout reduced from 5 to 3 seconds.
Zenyatta

Developer Comments: Harmony Orb can remain active on targets for extended periods with minimal upkeep. We're reducing its line-of-sight grace period to place greater emphasis on maintaining awareness of orb targets and repositioning it when needed.

Harmony Orb
  • Line of sight timeout reduced from 5 to 3 seconds.
