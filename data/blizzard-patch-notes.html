General Updates

Gameplay Sabotage Detection

We're improving our tools to combat gameplay sabotage in Overwatch. We have made improvements to our current detection tools that allow us to more accurately detect players engaging in disruptive behaviors, and we've already taken action against many players detected by these systems. Examples of gameplay sabotage include consistently and intentionally failing to contribute to team fights, repeatedly jumping off the map, and intentionally blocking team progress. We will also continue to iterate on these detection methods so that we can catch even more players who are intentionally making other players' experiences worse.

As part of this update, we have also rolled out 'Thank You' messages for reporting a player for disruptive behaviors. You will see these show up in the game's Notification Hub if you report a player for disruptive behaviors, and appropriate actions have been taken against them.

Reign of Talon - Season 5: A Grim Doctrine

New Event - Mystery Mayhem: Graveyard Games

A freakishly fun event rises from the grave -- Mystery Madness: Graveyard Games!

For four wicked weeks, complete challenges to earn ravishing rewards, and cast Offerings to help determine which shadowy characters are first to be summoned into a brand new limited-time mode.

Ghoulishly Good Challenges

Complete challenges to collect rewards. Every challenge conquered unearths spooky swag, as well as Offerings you can use in voting.

Offer Up Your Devotion

Offerings decide which characters crawl into the Graveyard Games mode next. Earn as many Offerings as you can, and direct them to any of the available heroes you want; you can spread them out, or pledge your allegiance to just one!

New Arcade Gamemode - Mystery Madness: Graveyard Games

Mystery Madness is our new Halloween Event mode, combining elements of Mystery Heroes, Junkenstein’s Lab, and Stadium. But there's an added twist: you gain Powers when you die. Mystery Madness is 5v5 Open Queue but with guaranteed 1-2-2 Role Composition and has the game format of Best-of-3 Control.

At the start of a match, you are assigned a random role and given 3 hero choices within that role. If you like to leave your chances up to fate, there is also a random button that will pick between 3 heroes and grant you a small boost.

Once the match starts, you will gain experience passively and from eliminations and assists. Once you gain enough experience, you will earn a level. When you die or on round transition, you will earn Power Choices, picking between 2 Powers, equal to the number of levels you accumulated since you last died. Powers are similar to Perks or Stadium Items / Powers, and can be either Common, Epic, or Legendary. Commons are comparable to Minor Perks or Rare to Epic Stadium items, and are usually supportive effects. Epics are comparable to Major Perks or Stadium Powers, and are usually the cornerstone of your build. Legendaries go even further beyond, and can lead to some truly crazy combinations. Some standout spooky powers are Jump Scare, which turns you into a Prop while out of combat, letting you flank and frighten your enemies, or Zomnic Summoner, which lets you throw a grenade that summons a Zomnic upon detonation. On death, you will also gain 10% Ultimate Charge and respawn with Overhealth equal to 40% of your Max Life and 30% Move Speed while out of combat.

Our newest spooky support, Doctrine, will also be playable in Mystery Madness: Graveyard Games!

Roadhog

Developer Comments: We had two main goals with this Roadhog rework: to reduce the frustration that comes out of how lethal Chain Hook ends up being on most heroes, and to give Roadhog a new tool to help him aid his team as a more traditional Tank would. Changing his primary fire to shoot in a 2-burst cadence alongside slight adjustments to Chain Hook should preserve the ability’s strength as a displacement tool while dampening its lethality. Meanwhile, Roadhog’s new ability, Trash Compactor, brings new utility for him and his team that feeds back into Roadhog being a lethal force on the battlefield. While Trash Compactor is active, Roadhog vacuums up projectiles in front of him, converting them into an explosive projectile to fling back at enemies. Operating as a strong defensive and offensive ability, Trash Compactor should help point Roadhog towards making game-swinging plays with more vectors than just trying to one-shot-combo heroes.

Developer Comments: Sombra is being brought into the Support role with this latest rework. In the past, her identity as a disruptive mastermind was at odds with the value a Damage hero was expected to bring. Moving Sombra to the Support role should allow us to fully lean into her hero fantasy while keeping important pillars of her gameplay intact.

One notable change of the rework is the removal of Hack as an ability. Facing Sombra often meant being unexpectedly locked out of your abilities from time to time. Core Game Updates

Advanced Info Panel Updates
  • Projectile Speed and Projectile Size have been added to all relevant weapons and abilities.

  • Various new keywords and line items have been added to describe more complex hero ability mechanics in more detail.

Protects
  • Tanks with Damage Reduction and Overhealth abilities are now more likely to get Protects when they're using these abilities and being targeted instead of nearby allies.

Ping System
  • Removed the text in the chat box that was added in Season 4 when pinging enemy heroes, since this often filled the chat box very quickly.
Mystery Heroes
  • The 3 Tank maximum has been restored.
Map Voting
  • It will now be impossible to be offered your last 2 maps played within a 2 hour period.
  • Escort and Hybrid will now be treated as the same mode for the purposes of offering at least two different game modes in Map Voting. This means that if two of the three visible maps are Escort and Hybrid, the third must be another mode. The random map does not follow these new rules and can still be any mode.

Stadium Updates

General Changes
  • Quick Play is now Open Queue but with role limits of 1 Tank, 2 Damage, and 3 Supports. 1 Tank is required.
  • Passive Health from Currency has been reduced by 50%.

Developer Comments: The Quick Play format change is aimed at lowering queue times, particularly for Support players in Stadium. This change is experimental and does not affect Competitive, and we will be keeping a close eye on it. This will also help inform what the best way to keep Stadium queue times sustainable for our Stadium players going forward is.

The passive health change is aimed at adding more tradeoffs to offensive itemization by giving less survivability out at baseline.
